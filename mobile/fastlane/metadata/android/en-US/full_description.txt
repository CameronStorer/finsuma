Finsuma is a playlist-first music player for your own Navidrome or Subsonic server. Stream your library from anywhere, with no ads, no tracking and no account other than your own server.<br>
<br>
<b>Features</b><br>
<ul>
<li><b>Auto DJ:</b> when your queue is about to run out, Finsuma adds songs like the one playing: similar songs first, then the same genre and artist. Choose songs or whole albums.</li>
<li><b>A queue that behaves:</b> "Play next" songs line up in the order you add them, shuffle starts from the current song, and new songs go to the end.</li>
<li><b>Car and headphones:</b> press play from your car or headphones even when the app is closed, and optionally start playing as soon as Bluetooth audio connects.</li>
<li><b>Playlists first:</b> ratings, pins and favorites for playlists, grid or list views, sorting, and quick search filters for songs, albums and artists.</li>
<li><b>Discovery:</b> tap a song for an instant mix; browse genres, random albums, recently added and most played.</li>
<li><b>Clean, dark design</b> with the album art blurred behind the player.</li>
<li>Lyrics, sleep timer, equalizer, replay gain, gapless playback, offline downloads, Android Auto, podcasts and internet radio.</li>
</ul>
<br>
<b>Optional: Finsuma server</b><br>
With the companion Finsuma server running next to your music server, you can save songs and playlists from YouTube and SoundCloud straight into your library, and keep playlist ratings and pins in sync with the Finsuma desktop app.<br>
<br>
Finsuma is free and open source (GPL-3.0). It builds on Tempus by eddyizm and its contributors.<br>
