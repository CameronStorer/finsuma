# Finsuma privacy policy

_Last updated: October 20, 2026_

Finsuma (the Android, Windows and Linux apps and the optional Finsuma server) does not collect,
sell or share any personal data with the developer or with third parties. There are no ads,
no analytics and no tracking.

## What the apps store and send

- **Your music server.** Finsuma connects only to the music server you add (for example
  Navidrome). Your server address, username and a password or token are stored on your device so
  the app can sign in. Everything you play, rate, favorite or add to playlists is sent to your
  own server and nowhere else.
- **The optional Finsuma server.** If you run it, the apps send it the YouTube or SoundCloud page
  you ask to download and your playlist ratings, pins and favorites. It runs on your own machine.
  It checks your sign-in with your music server.
- **On your device.** Settings, the play queue, cached artwork and audio, and any songs you choose
  to download stay on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Third-party services

Some features talk to third parties directly from your device, only when you use them:

- **YouTube and SoundCloud** pages shown inside the app (their own privacy policies apply).
- **Lyrics** lookups (LRCLIB) on the desktop app, when lyrics are enabled.
- **Software updates** on the desktop app are checked against Finsuma's GitHub releases.

## Permissions (Android)

- **Bluetooth (connect):** to notice when your car or headphones connect, so playback can start.
  No Bluetooth data leaves the device.
- **Notifications and foreground service:** to show playback controls while music plays.
- **Network:** to reach your music server.

## Children

Finsuma is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
